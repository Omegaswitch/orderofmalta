# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site, no runtime dependencies on the visitor's side (no client-side framework). HTML, CSS and JS are generated by a Python 3 build script (standard library and Pillow). Content is kept as files in this repository. Node is used only for the staff editor and the other serverless functions in `api/`. Hosting is Vercel.

## Users

Audiences, as far as the live site shows them. To be confirmed with the Grand Magistry.

- The general public, looking for the Order, its work and its history.
- Journalists, who use press releases, galleries and the press pages.
- Members of the Order and people looking for a contact, who use the contacts pages (regions and countries).
- Staff who publish news, press releases and galleries, through the editor at `/editor/`.

## Product Purpose

The official website of the Sovereign Military Hospitaller Order of St John of Jerusalem of Rhodes and of Malta, shown by its Grand Magistry. It explains the Order, its work and its governance, publishes news, press releases and galleries, and keeps the public archive of that material.

It replaces the WordPress site at www.orderofmalta.int with a static site that keeps the same pages, the same addresses and the same content in five languages.

Success: a visitor finds the page or item they came for, in their own language, at the same address as before, and the site loads fast and does not send visitors' data to third parties.

## Positioning

The Grand Magistry's own website. It is the official source for the Order's texts. Where the Order's own texts exist, they are the source. The rebuild does not rewrite official texts.

## Operating Context

- The live site is WordPress with the WPML plugin. It has five languages: English (default, no prefix), Italian (`/it/`), French (`/fr/`), German (`/de/`) and Spanish (`/es/`).
- Scouted on 15 Nov 2026 (`docs/build-spec.md`, section 0): about 2,257 English news items in total, 262 of them dated from 1 Jan 2025; press releases over 31 listing pages in English; galleries over 27 listing pages in English; publications over 13 pages. These are scouting numbers, not import counts.
- The site has a search, RSS feeds, a year archive of news, and YouTube videos. No Vimeo, Flickr or Issuu embeds were found in content from 2025 on.
- The live site loads Google Tag Manager, a pop-up for the app, and jQuery, Bootstrap and other scripts from the theme. The rebuild drops these.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: `en` (default, no prefix), `it`, `fr`, `de`, `es`. Every original path is kept, with the same language prefix. Each page links to its translation in the language switcher. A language without a translation shows no link to a translation.
- Time zone: Europe/Rome.
- Import scope for now: pages in all five languages, and news, press releases and galleries dated 1 Jan 2025 onward. Before launch either the full archive is imported or the old WordPress host is kept for old links (the launch gate in `docs/deployment.md`).
- Search runs in the browser, from one index per language.
- Embeds (YouTube, Vimeo) load only after a visitor clicks. Nothing is sent to a third party before that.
- No analytics, no tracking cookies and no cookie banner.
- Staff sign-in with Better Auth (e-mail and password, Postgres, roles owner, admin and editor, no self sign-up).
- The data-protection request form at `/gdpr/` sends the request by e-mail and stores nothing on the site.
- Emblems and brand files: only the official files in `docs/asset-manifest.md`. Nothing is redrawn or recoloured.
- The four-letter acronym of the Order's name is never written.

## Brand Commitments

- Only the brand files listed in `docs/asset-manifest.md` are used. The interim banner uses the official shield with live text. The outlined lockups are not yet approved (see `docs/brand-compliance.md`).
- Colours, type and layout follow the template's design system (`DESIGN.md`).

## Evidence on Hand

- The live site at www.orderofmalta.int: its WordPress REST API for pages, media and news (the news type is `notizie`), its sitemaps and its HTML pages. Press releases and galleries are not in the REST API. They are read from their listing and single pages.
- The build specification, `docs/build-spec.md`.
- The template: the Czech Grand Priory site at maltezskyrad.cz, its design system and its security model.

Absent, and not to be invented: approved lockup artwork in each language, the Grand Magistry's approval of this rebuild, the address that receives data-protection requests, and the legal details of the entity that runs the site.

## Product Principles

1. Same addresses: every original URL keeps working, in every language.
2. Official first: the Order's own texts and wording come from the Grand Magistry.
3. Truth over polish: import what is published, mark what is unknown, never invent.
4. Private by default: no third-party request before a visitor asks for it.
5. Maintainable by a small team: content is files, and the editor covers the daily work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA, as in the template. The audience includes older visitors and people with disabilities. Keyboard and screen-reader support, high contrast, clear type and reduced motion are required. Not yet tested on this site.
