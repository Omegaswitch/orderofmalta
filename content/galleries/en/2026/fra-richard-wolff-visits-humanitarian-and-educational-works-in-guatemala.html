On the occasion of his mission to Guatemala, Fra’ Richard Wolff, representing the Grand Master Fra’ John T. Dunlap, presided over the admission ceremony of two members to the Promise of Obedience and met with the local Order of Malta community. During his stay, he also visited some of the Order’s humanitarian and educational works in the country, including a social assistance medical center and a children’s institute.
