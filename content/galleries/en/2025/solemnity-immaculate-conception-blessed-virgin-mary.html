According to custom, on the Solemnity of the Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Grand Master Fra’ John T. Dunlap went to Piazza di Spagna in Rome, accompanied by members of the Sovereign Council, a representation of the Order of Malta’s Military Corps and Italian Relief Corps.
After the procession from the Magistral Palace, Fra’ John Dunlap laid a floral eight-pointed cross in homage to the statue of the Immaculate Conception.
The Papal Gendarmerie celebrated the Grand Master by playing the Order of Malta‘s anthem in a Via Condotti full of tourists and Romans.
This year, for the first time, Pope Leo XIV will also arrive in the afternoon. Welcomed by Cardinal Baldo Reina and Mayor Roberto Gualtieri, the Pontiff will stand in prayer in front of the monument and leave a garland of flowers.
