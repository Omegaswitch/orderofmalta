The Sovereign Order of Malta’s Grand Master Fra’ John Dunlap attended the Concert for the Year of Lithuanian Culture in Italy, in the presence of the President of the Italian Republic, Sergio Mattarella, and the President of the Republic of Lithuania, Gitanas Nausėda, who is on an official visit to Rome these days. The event, held in the Pauline Chapel of the Quirinal Palace, was attended by numerous diplomatic and institutional authorities.
