On the occasion of the 70th birthday of His Holiness Pope Leo XIV, the Grand Master Fra’ John Dunlap wished to express, on behalf of the Order of Malta, his gratitude and spiritual closeness. In his message, he underlined how “the motto of Your Holiness ‘in Illo uno unum’ may guide Peoples along the path of Unity and Peace, and towards those values of hospitality and brotherhood which, in these difficult times, are more essential than ever and which the Order of Malta has pursued since its very foundation.”
