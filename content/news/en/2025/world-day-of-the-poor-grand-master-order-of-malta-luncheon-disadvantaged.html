Some 1,440 people from the poorest sections of the population in France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Switzerland participated in a solidarity lunch organized by the Fratello network on the occasion of the World Day of the Poor and the Jubilee of the Poor.

Among the volunteers serving at the tables were the Order of Malta’s Grand Master, Fra’ John Dunlap, accompanied by the Ambassador to the Holy See, Antonio Zanardi Landi, and Sabina Zanardi Landi, who all had the honour of greeting His Holiness Pope Leo XIV.
