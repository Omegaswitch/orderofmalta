It is with deep regret that the Sovereign Military Order of Malta announces the resignation of the Grand Hospitaller, Fra’ Alessandro De Franciscis, which he handed in today for personal reasons.

The Grand Master of the Sovereign Order of Malta, Fra’ John Dunlap, and the entire Sovereign Council wish to thank Fra’ Sandro for his work since his election in January 2023 and for his great dedication to the Order of Malta and its activities.
