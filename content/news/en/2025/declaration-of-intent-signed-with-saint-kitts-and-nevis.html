The Grand Chancellor of the Order of Malta, Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo, signed a Declaration of Intent for opening diplomatic relations with Saint Kitts and Nevis. The signing took place on 24 September in New York, within the framework of the 80th session of the UN General Assembly underway this week.

In his meeting with Minister of Foreign Affairs Denzil Douglas, the Grand Chancellor stressed that the opening of diplomatic relations is intended to facilitate the implementation of humanitarian assistance projects on the ground. The Foreign Minister of Saint Kitts and Nevis stated he would promptly follow up on the Declaration of Intent with his Government, in order to proceed with the signing of the final agreement for opening international relations.
