The continuity of the Order of Malta’s assistance to the Ukrainian people was confirmed by Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo during his meeting with the Ukrainian Minister of Foreign Affairs, Andrii Sybiha, in New York.

The Grand Chancellor recalled the many forms of the Order of Malta’s activities in Ukraine, especially psychological support and physical rehabilitation.

For his part, the Foreign Minister expressed his great appreciation for the Order’s work in Ukraine and the gratitude of the Ukrainian authorities and people.
