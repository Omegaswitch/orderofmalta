In a bilateral meeting on 25 September in New York between Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo and the Prime Minister of Tonga, Aisake Valu Eke, the initiation of diplomatic relations and humanitarian initiatives of common interest were discussed.

In expressing his appreciation for the Order of Malta’s mission and activities, the Prime Minister said he was committed to continuing the dialogue and cooperation between Tonga and the Order of Malta.
