From 24 to 31 October, Rome welcomed the Order of Malta Austria’s pilgrimage, which gathered 450 participants, including over 150 sick, elderly and disabled people. The initiative was organized by Malteser Hospitaldienst Austria, the Order of Malta’s Hospital Assistance Service in the country.

The pilgrimage offered an important occasion for sharing and prayer, also enabling those needing continuing assistance to have an experience of faith in the Eternal City. Thanks to the commitment of the Order’s volunteers, participants were given personalized support that included help with travel and everyday activities, as well as medical and nursing care.

The programme included liturgical celebrations, moments of prayer and visits to some of Christianity’s major sites, including St. Peter’s Basilica, the Sistine Chapel, the Pantheon, St. John Lateran Basilica and Castel Gandolfo. Pilgrims also visited the Order of Malta’s Magistral Villa on the Aventine Hill. One of the pilgrimage’s most significant moments was participation in Pope Leo XIV’s general audience in St. Peter’s Square.

The journey testified to the centrality of the spiritual dimension and service to others in the Order of Malta’s mission. A commitment fundamentally expressed in the consideration of the dignity and needs of each person, promoting the participation in religious and community life also of those in particularly vulnerable conditions.

Founded in 1956 by the Order of Malta, Malteser Hospitaldienst Austria now has over a thousand volunteers active in caring for the elderly, sick and disabled, in emergency services and in disaster-relief efforts.
