On the 75th° anniversary of diplomatic relations between the Sovereign Military Order of Malta and Paraguay, Grand Master Fra’ John T. Dunlap met with the President of the Republic, Santiago Peña, yesterday in Asunción. The visit represents a new stage in the consolidation of bilateral relations, initiated in 1951 and reinforced in recent years through high-level institutional meetings. These include President Peña’s official visit to Rome in 2023, during which the head of state invited the Grand Master to make an official visit to Paraguay.

President Peña highlighted the depth of the solid and long-standing relations between Paraguay and the Order. The Grand Master’s visit on the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations has thus a strong symbolic value. The President also expressed his willingness to strengthen the partnership with the Order of Malta and to initiate new collaborations in Paraguay.

During the official visit, the Grand Master also laid a wreath at the Panteón Nacional de los Héroes in Asunción, paying homage to Paraguay’s national heroes.

In the meeting at the Presidential Palace, Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo and Minister for Foreign Affairs Rubén Ramírez Lezcano signed a Cooperation Agreement, which will enable them to examine and agree on new initiatives with a social impact in the country.

In the context of of the institutional meetings, Grand Hospitaller Josef D. Blotz held talks with the Minister for Public Health and Social Welfare, Isaías Ricardo Fretes, to discuss healthcare activities and possible areas of collaboration, with particular regard to the mental health sector, including within the context of ministerial programmes promoting health in schools.

In Asunción, the Grand Master met with members and volunteers of the National Association, active since 2004 in health and dental care, with particular attention to young people and the most vulnerable. Last year, the Association signed an agreement with Montero Medical Missions to develop joint programmes in several areas, including orthopaedics, ophthalmology, dentistry, child nutrition, mental health and hearing assistance.

The Order’s commitment in the country is carried out through a number of social and healthcare initiatives. Activities include the distribution of food, medicines and basic necessities, as well as providing medical and dental care to indigenous communities and support to families in need. In the Puerto Pabla and Lambaré areas, meals are distributed daily to the most vulnerable elderly, whilst the Maltese Emergency Service (SEMA) and the Order of Malta’s Volunteer Corps (CVOM) are engaged in healthcare provision and first-aid training. Furthermore, every year, the Order provides assistance to thousands of pilgrims during the celebrations of Our Lady of Caacupé.

The official visits to Paraguay and Chile are part of the Grand Master’s greater journey to Latin America, which began in Argentina. An itinerary that confirms the Order of Malta’s focus on the continent and its desire to strengthen dialogue with local institutions and realities, supporting the development of its humanitarian mission.
