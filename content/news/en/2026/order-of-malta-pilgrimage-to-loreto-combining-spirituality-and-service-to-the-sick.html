The Sovereign Military Order of Malta’s traditional Pilgrimage to the Holy House of Loreto has concluded, bringing together approximately 1,100 participants from October 23 to 25, including some 120 Our Lords the Sick from all the Order’s Italian delegations.

Led by Fra’ Nicola Tegoni, President of the Italian Pilgrimages Foundation, the journey was an important opportunity for encounter, prayer and sharing under the sign of the spirituality in the service to the sick mission that characterizes the Order of Malta.

Among the participants were the Grand Prior of Rome Fra’ Roberto Viazzo, the Grand Prior of Naples and Sicily Fra’ Nicolò Custoza de’ Cattani and the Procuratore of the Grand Priory of Lombardy and Venice, Bernardo dei Conti Gambaro. Also present were four novices of the Order, accompanied by Fra’ Tom Mulligan.

The pilgrimage began on Friday 23 October, with Holy Mass presided over by Msgr. Fabio Dal Cin, Archbishop Prelate of Loreto and Pontifical Delegate for the Sanctuary of the Holy House, followed by the Procession and Eucharistic blessing. In the evening, after the recitation of the Rosary, the greeting of Fra’ Nicola Tegoni and the presentation of the Medaglie Lauretane, the pilgrims experienced one of the most significant moments of the programme with the passage through the Holy House.

Saturday 24 October was marked by liturgical celebrations, the Stations of the Cross in the basilica and moments of reflection and instruction dedicated to young people and children. In the afternoon, Monsignor Fabio Dal Cin offered a reflection on Loreto’s spiritual message. In the evening, the Rosary Meditations and the traditional Torchlight Procession, led by the Capuchin Friars of the Sanctuary, brought the participants together in a moment of intense prayer and recollection.

On Sunday 15 October, the Eucharistic Concelebration presided over by Msgr. Maurizio Stefanutti, followed by the Eucharistic Procession and Blessing, concluded the three days of pilgrimage.

Fundamental, as every year, was the contribution of healthcare personnel and numerous volunteers, particularly young people, who accompanied and assisted the Our Lords the Sick during the celebrations and activities, testifying to the Order of Malta’s spirit of service and fraternity.
