Relations between the Sovereign Military Order of Malta and the Principality of Andorra continue to strengthen, following the establishment of bilateral ties in June 2025.

On the occasion of the Munich Security Conference, held on Friday 13 February, Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo and Minister of Foreign Affairs Imma Tor signed a Memorandum of Understanding for international cooperation.

During the meeting, several project proposals previously submitted by the Order of Malta—currently under review by the Principality of Andorra—were discussed.
