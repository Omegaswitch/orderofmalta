The 10th Conference of the Americas of the Sovereign Military Order of Malta opened in Buenos Aires, Argentina, bringing together over 100 representatives of the Order’s associations, embassies, and humanitarian organizations present on the continent. “One Mission, Many Vocations – the Order of Malta in the Americas Between Tradition and Modernity” is the theme of the meeting, dedicated to the challenges and prospects of the Order’s mission in the region.

The meeting was opened by Grand Master Fra’ John T. Dunlap, who during his visit to Argentina will meet with the President of the Republic Javier Milei at the Casa Rosada on 26th October. “As members of the Order of Malta, we all share one single purpose or mission: to serve the sick and the poor,” said the Grand Master. “Even our commitment to ‘defend the Faith’ should be understood as being accomplished through service to the poor and sick. For it is only by serving the poor and helpless of this world that we defend Catholic Christianity from its enemies. A vocation to the Order of Malta is, therefore, a calling from God to live a life dedicated to the poor and sick. By so doing, we find our personal salvation through the Order itself.” The Grand Master then stressed that the vocation to the Order represents a call to dedicate one’s life to the service of the most vulnerable, in which the spiritual and charitable dimensions are deeply united.

Grand Commander Fra’ Emmanuel Rousseau also dwelt on the meaning of the Order’s charism and vocation, stressing that “For us as Hospitallers, tuitio fidei is a profoundly personal spiritual dimension, nurtured in the intimacy of our relationship with God, which shapes our very way of being and finds expression in our daily actions, particularly in the exercise of obsequium pauperum.” At the heart of Fra’ Emmanuel Rousseau’s speech was also the Order of Malta’s role within the Church, an ecclesiological reality inspired by the Holy Spirit and a religious community inserted into the universal Catholic family.

Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo emphasized the importance of promoting greater involvement of association members in local activities, delineating a ‘decalogue’ dedicated to the fundamental approach that must guide the organization of initiatives, fundraising and international partnerships. “Collaborating with other humanitarian organizations, foundations, public institutions and private entities means being able to multiply the effectiveness of our initiatives,” the Grand Chancellor said, with the intention of setting out a standpoint for the coming years. He then also listed the different instruments offered by the Grand Magistry to support local activities, starting with the Order of Malta Donors for Projects Foundation.

Grand Hospitalier Josef D. Blotz outlined the main humanitarian activities worldwide and especially some recent interventions, including the response to the emergency caused by the earthquake in Venezuela, a concrete example of coordination and collaboration between various associations and relief bodies. The theme of synergy was also taken up by Fra’ Francis Vassallo, Receiver of the Common Treasure, who highlighted the need for a cultural shift in funding management and fundraising activities to foster greater integration and sustainability of initiatives.

The inaugural day, initiated by a speech by Jorge Ortiz, President of the Order of Malta’s Argentine Association, was also attended by Agustín Ezequiel Caulo, Secretary for Worship and Civilization of the Argentine Republic. Thanking the latter for his presence, the Grand Master recalled the deep and historic ties of friendship between the Order of Malta and Argentina, hoping for new opportunities for collaboration on the humanitarian and diplomatic levels. Themes that will be explored in the upcoming official meetings between the Grand Chancellor and Foreign Minister Pablo Quirno Magrane and between the Grand Hospitaller and Human Capital Minister Sandra Pettovello.

Also enriching the agenda of the conference will be visits to some social projects of the Argentine association, involving in food distribution and social reintegration initiatives for the most marginalized sections of the population.
