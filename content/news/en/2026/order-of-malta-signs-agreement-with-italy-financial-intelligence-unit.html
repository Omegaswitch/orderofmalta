The Sovereign Military Order of Malta’s Receiver of the Common Treasure, Fra’ Francis J. Vassallo, today signed a collaboration agreement with the Financial Intelligence Unit for Italy (FIU). The agreement was signed by UIF Director Enzo Serata for the Bank of Italy.

The partnership with the Bank of Italy’s Unit is aimed at strengthening financial analysis tools, compliance with international anti-money laundering standards and the prevention of economic and financial risks related, in particular, to the management of potential donations and funding to support the Order of Malta’s humanitarian activities.

The agreement is part of the Order’s commitment to promoting the highest standards of integrity, transparency and accountability in the management of financial resources, with regards to its global humanitarian projects.
