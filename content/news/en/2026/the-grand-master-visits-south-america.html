The Grand Master of the Sovereign Military Order of Malta, Fra’ John T. Dunlap, who started his tour in Argentina in the Southern Cone, met with President Javier Milei yesterday at the Casa Rosada. During the talks, the excellent bilateral relations were confirmed with Argentina, the first country outside Europe with which the Order had established diplomatic relations in 1948. President Milei also expressed his appreciation for the Order’s humanitarian activities in Argentina, focused on social inclusion and vocational training.

A further step in strengthening relations was taken with the signing of the Cooperation Agreement between Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo and the Argentine Minister of Foreign Affairs, Pablo Quirno. In his speech, the Argentine Foreign Minister underlined the fact that the Order’s hospitaller tradition spans the centuries, maintaining its relevance intact “beyond the methods of every era”. Both the parties confirmed they shared fundamental values, and especially assistance to the most vulnerable segments of the population, which form the basis of the Order’s humanitarian initiatives in Argentina and worldwide. During the talks, the Grand Chancellor also outlined the Order’s commitment to emergency response, citing recent interventions in Venezuela and Colombia following the earthquakes.

The meetings in Argentina take place at the end of the 10th Conference of the Americas that opened on 22 October in Buenos Aires. The initiative brought together the presidents of the National Associations, representatives of the Order’s Diplomatic Missions in North, Central and South America and heads of the Order’s government, providing an important opportunity for discussion on the institution’s activities and future prospects on the continent. There are 25 National Associations in the Americas while the Order maintains diplomatic relations with 27 countries in the region and official relations with Canada.

In Buenos Aires, the Grand Master inaugurated a new professional training programme in gastronomy and hospitality to create jobs, thanks to the contribution of the Order of Malta Foundation – Donors for Projects (OMDP). The aim is social support and reintegration in employment for Argentine citizens who are homeless and living in poverty and for immigrants from Venezuela, Paraguay, Peru and Bolivia living in the Buenos Aires metropolitan area.

In Argentina, the Order promotes numerous initiatives in the social, health and educational fields, through volunteer activities and the distribution of basic necessities, targeted contributions to hospital facilities and paediatric wards, projects to care for vulnerable people and first-aid training programmes. The deep roots in Argentina’s social fabric are also visible in healthcare and logistics facilities during important religious pilgrimages.

In Buenos Aires, Grand Hospitaller Josef D. Blotz met with the Minister of Human Capital, Sandra Viviana Pettovello, at the Human Capital Training Centre, a facility promoted by the Argentine Government which, through collaboration between the public and private sectors, offers vocational training and technical qualification courses to young people and adults, with the aim of fostering integration in the working world and promoting economic autonomy. Possibilities for future collaboration were explored.

After Argentina, the Grand Master will pay an official visit to Paraguay and Chile. Latin America is of crucial importance to the Order, as emphasized last January by Grand Master Fra’ John T. Dunlap in his address to the Diplomatic Corps accredited to the Order of Malta.
