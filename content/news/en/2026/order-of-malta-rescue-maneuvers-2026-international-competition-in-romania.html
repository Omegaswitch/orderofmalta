The third edition of the Order of Malta’s international rescue competition brought together 14 teams from six European countries in Maramureș, promoting emergency preparedness, international cooperation and the exchange of expertise.

The participating teams included representatives of the Order of Malta’s relief organisations from Germany, Poland, Ukraine, Lithuania, Albania and Romania, alongside Romanian emergency response authorities and local volunteer services.

Over four days, participants took part in realistic emergency and rescue scenarios, testing their technical skills, coordination and ability to work effectively under pressure, across language barriers and different operational procedures.

Beyond the competition, the Rescue Maneuvers offered an opportunity for practical training, the sharing of best practices and stronger operational cooperation among the Order of Malta’s relief organisations and their emergency response partners.

The competition highlighted the teams’ professionalism and spirit of solidarity, reaffirming the importance of training and volunteer commitment in fulfilling the Order of Malta’s humanitarian mission to protect lives and assist those in need.
