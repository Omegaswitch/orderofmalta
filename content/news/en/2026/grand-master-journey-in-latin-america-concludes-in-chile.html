The Grand Master of the Sovereign Military Order of Malta, Fra’ John Dunlap, concluded his visit to Latin America in Santiago de Chile.

The Grand Master was able to experience numerous humanitarian activities at close quarters, starting with the Respiratory Rehabilitation Centre at San José Hospital where, since 2006, the Auxilio Maltés Foundation has contributed to financing renovations and supplying medical equipment for patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and other cardiac, neurological and respiratory conditions. The visit continued at Roberto del Río Hospital, another key hub for the Chilean Relief Corps’ activities, where the Order provides regular transportation for children requiring oxygen therapy. The activities in the two hospitals enable the Foundation to assist hundreds of oxygen-dependent patients, with over 35,000 services and annual consultations.

In Chépica, central Chile – which the delegation visited on Tuesday 3 November – the Auxilio Maltés Foundation runs rehabilitation programmes for neurodivergent children and the elderly. Following the earthquake that hit the town, the foundation also helped remove the rubble and construct the “Mercado Maltés”, a community space for entrepreneurs and local residents. For 30 years, the Foundation has played a leading role in social health care and emergency response in the country, reaching approximately 3,000 people annually thanks to a network of 311 volunteers.

During his stay in Santiago, Fra’ John Dunlap met with Cardinal Fernando Natalio Chomali Garib, Archbishop of Santiago.

Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo also gave a lecture at the Pontificia Universidad Católica, addressed to students and dedicated to the Order of Malta’s thousand-year history, the global dimension of its humanitarian and diplomatic actions as well as the mission’s future challenges.

The Grand Master’s journey in Latin America, which touched Argentina, Paraguay and finally Chile, was an important occasion to strengthen diplomatic relations with the three countries and reaffirm the Order of Malta’s commitment in the region through support for humanitarian and social initiatives and collaboration with local institutions.
