what can i find in the app

The Order of Malta App gives you instant access to our latest news, inspiring stories of humanitarian service, immersive virtual tours of our historic sites, a comprehensive calendar of events and initiatives worldwide and a dedicated section on spirituality.

Is the app only for members of the Order of Malta?

No, the public section is open to everyone. A private section offers additional resources dedicated to members and staff who hold a professional or institutional role within the Order of Malta.

What is the mission of the Order of Malta today?

The Sovereign Order is a lay religious order of the Catholic Church since 1113 and a subject of international law. It has diplomatic relations with over 100 states and the European Union, and permanent observer status at the United Nations. It is neutral, impartial and apolitical.
Today, the Order of Malta is active in more than 130 countries caring for people in need through its medical, social and humanitarian works. Day-to-day, its broad spectrum of social projects provides a constant support for forgotten or excluded members of society. It is especially involved in helping people living in the midst of armed conflicts and natural disasters.
