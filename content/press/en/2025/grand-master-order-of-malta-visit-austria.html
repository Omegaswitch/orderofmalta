Vienna, 28 November 2025 – The Grand Master of the Sovereign Order of Malta, Fra’ John Dunlap, on an official visit to Austria, was received today by Federal President Alexander Van der Bellen at the Hofburg, the official residence of the Austrian President, where a private meeting between the two Heads of State took place.

Followed by a working session with their respective delegations, the meeting reaffirmed the excellent relations at all levels between Austria and the Sovereign Order of Malta—among the longest-standing in the Order’s history.

During the discussions, Fra’ John Dunlap emphasized the shared commitment to support the most vulnerable: “Today’s meeting demonstrates the fruitful co-operation between Austria and the Order of Malta. The Order’s mission to care for the most vulnerable worldwide is more pertinent than ever. Its universal principles of solidarity and the protection of human life remain steadfast. We are fully aware that we share such a vision with the Republic of Austria.” President Van der Bellen highlighted the historical depth of relations between Austria and the Order of Malta, expressing great appreciation for the humanitarian activities carried out in crisis contexts such as Ukraine and Gaza, as well as for the social and healthcare initiatives developed in Austria.

A key moment of the day was the joint visit by the Grand Master, President Van der Bellen, and the First Lady to the Malteser Ordenshaus, the modern care home for the elderly and the sick run by the Order of Malta, located in the heart of Vienna and inaugurated in 2022. Equipped with state-of-the-art care services, the facility offers 72 permanent care places and ensures a familiar and dignified environment for its residents. The care home features modern apartments, shared spaces such as a library, a historic refectory, a gym, treatment rooms, and a professional care service available 24 hours a day. After meeting residents, staff, and volunteers, the Grand Master and the President unveiled a commemorative plaque, underscoring their shared commitment to supporting the most vulnerable.

On the occasion of the visit, the Grand Chancellor, Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo, and the Federal Minister for the EU, Integration and Family, Claudia Plakolm, signed a Memorandum of Understanding. The Memorandum includes cooperation initiatives promoting religious freedom and development projects in third countries, further strengthening the partnership between Austria and the Order of Malta and their joint contribution in the international arena.

The day concluded with a meeting between the Grand Master and the Mayor and Governor of Vienna, Dr. Michael Ludwig, during which they discussed cooperation with the city and the role of the Order’s institutions in providing social and health services to the most vulnerable citizens.

A collaboration rooted in centuries – The Order of Malta in Austria

Austria is home to one of the oldest establishments of the Order of Malta: the Grand Priory of Austria, dating back to the 12th century. Today, it is complemented by the work of the Malteser Hospitaldienst Austria, founded in 1956, which is active in the fields of medical, social and disaster relief. With over 2,200 volunteers who are also actively helping in the recent crises in Ukraine and the Middle East, the Malteser Hospitaldienst Austria is one of the operational pillars of the Order of Malta’s mission. In addition, the Order’s activities in Austria include the modern care home Malteser Ordenshaus, Maltese Care mobile care service, and Maltese Children’s Aid, which runs the Hilde Umdasch House for children and young people with life-shortening diseases.
