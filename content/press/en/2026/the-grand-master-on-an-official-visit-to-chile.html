Santiago de Chile, 2 November 2026 – The Grand Master of the Sovereign Military Order of Malta, Fra’ John Dunlap’s official trip to Latin America concludes in Chile. He was received by President José Antonio Kast Rist on Friday, 30 October at the Moneda Palace in Santiago de Chile. The meeting, followed by a working session with the respective delegations, confirmed the excellent state of bilateral relations initiated in 1955. President Kast spoke of a “historic” visit, expressing his appreciation for the Order’s activities in the country. The two parties indicated their shared desire to strengthen cooperation in the area of social health care and natural disasters, through plans to be explored and defined in the coming months.

During the talks, the Grand Master extended his best wishes to the President for the success of his mandate, begun just a few months ago, hoping for the new administration’s particular commitment to human dignity and people in conditions of great fragility. Fra’ John Dunlap also invited the President to visit the Magistral Palace in Rome.

The official visit was also an occasion for further institutional meetings: the Grand Hospitaller, Josef D. Blotz, met with the Minister of Health, May Chomali, while the Secretary General for Foreign Affairs, Giampaolo Cantini, had a meeting with the Secretary General for Foreign Affairs of Chile, Frank Tressler. Among the topics addressed were the Order’s humanitarian activities in Gaza and Bethlehem and the prospects for collaboration in natural emergency management in Chile and the region. Instead, Grand Chancellor Riccardo Paternò di Montecupo will give a speech to the students of the Pontificia Universidad Católica today.

During the day, the Grand Master will visit the Pulmonary Rehabilitation Centre at San José Hospital, where, since 2006, the Order has contributed to the financing of renovations and the supply of medical equipment for patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and other cardiac, neurological and respiratory conditions. After the unveiling of a commemorative plaque, the visit will continue at the Roberto del Río Hospital, where the Order provides regular transportation for children needing oxygen therapy.

The Order of Malta’s presence in Chile is notable for its wide-ranging commitment in the humanitarian sector. The National Association is active in the distribution of food, basic necessities and fundraising, while the National Relief Corps, the Auxilio Maltés Foundation, has played a crucial role for 30 years in both social welfare and response to national emergencies, such as the earthquakes and fires that have struck the country. Founded in 1996 to address the urgent need to treat people with serious lung diseases caused by viruses, tobacco use, the consequences of tuberculosis and exposure to chemicals, among other things, Auxilio Maltés supports hospitals with limited resources and provides support to the elderly, children with disabilities, and families in need.

The Order of Malta’s activities in Chile testify to the value of close collaboration between its various national entities, united in their commitment to the most vulnerable and in their devotion to their mission of serving the poor and sick.
